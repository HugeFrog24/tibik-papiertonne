# Klingelsturm — design

Date: 2026-11-03. Status: design approved in conversation, spec awaiting review.

## Goal

The film loses grip after „ICH MELDE SIE DER HAUSVERWALTUNG!". This act makes
Karen persistent and turns the tables on her: she follows Tibik home, besieges
his door, escalates by phone to the Hausverwaltung and finally to 110, where
the dispatcher has the last word. A silent coda shows that the box was worth
saving.

## Settled decisions

- Escalation goes up to a 110 call. The dispatcher is heard (as speech
  bubbles from the phone), never seen. Misusing 110 is a real offence
  (§ 145 StGB), so the rule-enforcer ends up as the one breaking a real rule.
- Karen is **Frau Schulz**, a resident: **1. OG rechts**, directly across the
  landing from Tibik (1. OG links). The pair of shoes on the opposite doormat
  are hers. She enters Hausmuell from the street because she is coming home.
- Tibik does not recognise her at first (anonymous Wohnblock); she tells him
  who she is.
- Structure A: Hausmuell gains the exchange and the exit; a **hard cut** (a
  declared time jump) to the new scene „Klingelsturm" on the 1. OG landing.
  Karen is never animated on stairs.
- The **last spoken word** is the dispatcher's. After it, the stairwell light
  times out on Karen. A **silent coda** follows (no dialogue).
- Proof that the box is useful, **both** ways: an intercut of Tibik calmly
  packing a parcel in his Flur during the siege, and the coda in which he
  walks out with the finished parcel.
- Runner (rule of three): Tibik keeps to „der Umwelt zuliebe"; Karen keeps
  telling him to buy a new one.
- Doorbell: an **electronic two-tone chime that re-triggers while the button
  is held** (Sturmklingeln).
- Spyhole shot: a **true fisheye** (barrel distortion), not just a wide lens.
- Tone stays as before: Tibik friendly and professional; jokes are about
  Karen's behaviour, never her body or age. Strangers say Sie.

## Beats and dialogue

Speech is shown as bubbles, as in the existing scenes; Karen shouts in
capitals. Timings are first estimates, tuned when blocking.

One phrase per bubble (user, 2026-11-03): a line of several sentences below
plays as consecutive bubbles. Every bubble stays up at least 5/6 s and long
enough to read at 20 characters per second; its type is at least 46 px at
1080p and fits inside its bubble (checked against the fonts' real glyph
widths). Karen shouts every bubble, her hesitation too („…NEIN."; user,
2026-11-03: a quiet bubble broke her continuity).

### Coda (silent)

In the dark, his lock clicks and the door opens; warm light from his Flur
falls on Karen. Tibik steps out with the box taped up as a parcel (address
label, not legible). He presses the landing light switch for her (the light
comes back), gives a friendly nod, and goes down the stairs with the parcel.
Her shoes stand on her mat. (2026-11-06) She pockets her phone; close-up: a
cigarette in the corner of her mouth, a red lighter in her right hand, she
lights it and pockets the lighter. Cut, about three minutes later: she draws
on the half-smoked cigarette, the timer light goes out on her again, only the
ember glows. End.

## Staging and cameras

The camera stays on the stair side of the line between the two doors.

1. **Wide**: the existing „His door" set-up beside her door; Karen from three
   quarters behind.
2. **Insert**: her thumb on his bell button, held down.
3. **Spyhole**: the lens sits 1 cm outside the door leaf at the spyhole,
   looking out; rendered with a barrel-distortion pass inside a round,
   vignetted frame.
4. **Medium** for the calls, from the top of the flight; phone bubbles get a
   jagged „electric" tail pointing at the phone.
5. **Flur** intercuts: inside his flat, a side-on medium on Tibik at the chest
   of drawers, the flat door and the chime unit in the background.
6. **Wide final** and the coda from the wide set-up.

## Sets and props

- **Tibik's Flur** (new interior on the existing Flur floor, about
  2.0 × 2.2 m): walls and ceiling, a warm ceiling light, the inside of his
  door (lever, spyhole), the doorbell chime unit above the door, the
  intercom handset by the door, coat hooks, a chest of drawers. The interior
  is seen only in the intercuts and through the open door in the coda.
- **Mat-aware ground**: the landing's ground height includes the doormats
  (2 cm). Karen stands on Tibik's mat to ring.
- **Big-button phone** (about 12 × 5.5 × 1.6 cm) with a drawn keypad; the
  screen shows the digits as she dials.
- **Parcel**: the existing Karton closed and taped, with an address label,
  plus a used book and a tape dispenser for the intercut.

## Sound

Everything on the landing gets the stairwell's long reverb; the chime is
heard muffled through his door on the landing and dry in his Flur.
Synthesised deterministically (like `scripts/make-ambience.mjs`) unless a
CC0 Kenney file fits.

- Electronic two-tone chime, re-triggered for as long as the button is held.
- DTMF per key: 1 = 697 + 1209 Hz, 0 = 941 + 1336 Hz.
- German ringing tone (Freiton/Rufton): 425 Hz, 1 s on, 4 s off.
- Busy tone after the hang-up: 425 Hz, 480 ms on, 480 ms off (secondary
  sources; one older configuration uses 500/500).
- Answering-machine beep (about 1 kHz), Karen's panting, tape screech, the
  timer relay click when the light goes out (existing switch sound), lock and
  door sounds (existing).

## Light timer

Tibik switched the light on downstairs as they came in, off screen inside the
time jump. The timer duration is documented in CONTINUITY.md, lies within the
0.5–20 min that real stairwell timers offer, and is chosen so that the light
expires right after the dispatcher's line. Before going out it gives the
switch-off warning (dim or flicker) that DIN 18015-2 asks for. In the coda
Tibik restarts it with the landing switch.

## Continuity

- The rule „each scene starts exactly where the previous one ended" stays.
  The Hausmuell → Klingelsturm cut is its first **declared time jump**: a
  named exception the continuity test knows about, not a weakened rule.
- At the start of Klingelsturm: Tibik is inside with the box, his door shut
  and latched, lever at rest; Karen is on the landing; her shoes on her mat.
- The coda's descent reuses the preamble's door, switch and stair motions;
  Tibik carries the parcel the way he carries the box elsewhere.

## Tests

No clipping, outlines included, as everywhere else.

- **Ground**: on a mat, feet rest on the mat top. First check whether
  Tibik's *drawn* soles already sink into his mat in the existing film (his
  collider ends exactly at the mat top); if so, fix it with a test.
- **Contacts**: Karen's thumb touches the bell button while she holds it
  (same contact test as Tibik's light-switch press); Tibik's hand on the
  switch in the coda.
- **Clipping**: Karen's body, hands and phone against walls, doors, railing,
  mats and each other; Tibik, parcel, book and tape in the Flur; the parcel on
  the stairs.
- **Cameras**: no camera in or against a wall; the spyhole lens 1 cm outside
  the door leaf.
- **Continuity**: the declared time jump; the start state above.
- **Dialogue**: lines in order, never overlapping, Sie-form throughout; the
  last spoken line is the dispatcher's; 110 is dialled before the police
  bubble; the runner appears three times.
- **Sound** (from data): the chime repeats exactly while the button is held;
  each dialled digit plays its DTMF pair; ringing and busy tone cadences; the
  relay click at the light-out.
- **Light**: timer within range, warning before darkness, back on after the
  coda's press.
- **Fisheye**: unit test of the distortion mapping (centre fixed, radius
  monotonic, round frame) plus stills checked by eye.

## Build order

Small iterations, each shown before the next (the user's preferred way):

1. Spike: fisheye pass in `ThreeCanvas` (render target plus a full-screen
   barrel shader, three.js only, no new dependency). If it cannot work
   deterministically in Remotion, fall back to a wide lens with a round
   vignette and say so.
2. Mat-aware ground, plus the Tibik sole check.
3. Hausmuell extension (lines 2–7, Tibik's exit, Karen storming after).
4. Klingelsturm blocking: Karen at the door, bell press and hold, cameras,
   bubbles, chime.
5. Phone: prop, pocket, calls, DTMF, ringing and busy tones, phone bubbles.
6. Tibik's Flur set and the packing intercuts.
7. Light-out and the silent coda.
8. Film assembly, CONTINUITY.md, final review.

## Out of scope

- Police or any third character on screen.
- Karen climbing the stairs on screen (possible later iteration: the Haustür
  closing in her face).
- Where Tibik keeps his door key (still the user's call; the cut avoids it).
- Real brands, real operators, legible real addresses.

## Later (optional, after this act)

- Karen's sloppy-speaking spray in the Hausmuell exchange (the user's idea): tiny
  droplets on her loudest syllables — not a deliberate spit — falling on the
  pavement and on Tibik's box. It is about her behaviour, not her body.

## Research notes

- § 145 StGB punishes intentional or knowing misuse of emergency calls;
  calls to 110/112 count as a criminal offence, not a regulatory one
  (gesetze-im-internet.de; secondary summaries).
- German ringing tone 425 Hz, 1 s / 4 s; busy 425 Hz, 480 / 480 ms
  (itwissen.info, Asterisk configurations; the official TR 110-1 was not
  checked).
- Stairwell timers adjustable 0.5–20 min (ABB E232); DIN 18015-2 switch-off
  warning (ledger, pass 22).
- Doormats in the stairwell are generally allowed; permanent shoes or racks
  generally not (AG Köpenick 4 C 143/17, via consumer-law summaries) — which
  is what makes Karen's shoes a quiet joke.
