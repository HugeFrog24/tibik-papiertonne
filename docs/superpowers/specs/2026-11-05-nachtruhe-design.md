# Nachtruhe — design

Date: 2026-11-05. Status: design approved in conversation (five sections),
spec awaiting review.

## Goal

A breather between the Klingelsturm and the epilogue, so the film has room
to breathe after the stairwell standoff. Wordless, about 29 s. Tibik comes
home at dusk, goes to bed, the camera rises over a sleeping Musterstadt while
Karen's window stays lit longest, and he wakes up on the rainy morning two
weeks later. One sleep carries the time jump; no day repeats.

## Settled decisions

- **Shape (user):** the dusk exterior, the drone rise over the roofs and a
  bedroom, all three combined. Artistic liberty on time: one night stands for
  the two weeks. No repeated sunrises, no day-by-day montage.
- **Order (user):** dusk wide → bedroom, evening → drone rise at night with
  one grey dawn → bedroom, morning (the rainy day two weeks later) → the
  epilogue's opening wide.
- **Caption (user):** "Zwei Wochen später." moves to the bedroom morning,
  where the time actually jumps, and comes off the epilogue's opening wide.
- **Name:** "Nachtruhe" — the night, and the Hausordnung's quiet hours.
- **The bedroom faces the street,** so the lamp going out inside and his
  window going dark outside are one moment (a small liberty; bedrooms often
  face the courtyard).
- **No roller shutter in the bedroom (user):** his bedroom window has none,
  on the facade too, in every scene.
- **Flat roofs (user)** on No. 12, No. 13 and the neighbouring blocks.
- **Format (user):** build in 16:9 as now. The release may become 9:16 with
  voices instead of bubbles and captions; keep each new shot's key action near
  the frame centre so a later vertical reframe is easy.
- **No duplicated general code (user's rule):** the street, the house, the
  lighting, the rain, the ambience, Tibik's rig and gait come from the shared
  pieces every scene uses, extended through props.
- **Everyday imperfections:** new objects follow docs/CONTINUITY.md,
  "Everyday imperfections" — subtle marks of use, never staged; new ideas are
  parked for the user.

## The bedroom

A narrow room in Tibik's flat (1. OG links, west of the stairwell), its short
far wall on the street facade; its window is one of that flat's first-floor
street windows (the plan pins which).

- **Shell:** white walls, white textured ceiling with a smoke alarm
  (Rauchmelder, required in German bedrooms) and a plain round glass dome
  ceiling lamp; wood-look laminate with white skirting; a wood-veneer door at
  the near end, off his Flur.
- **Window wall:** one big single-sash Dreh-Kipp window with the radiator
  under it, both behind two curtain panels in a solid warm sand colour that
  meet at the window's middle, on a rail with a wood-look pelmet; each panel
  stacks at its own wall. The curtains are translucent enough to glow with
  daylight. The gap between the bed and the curtains (47 cm) is too narrow for
  him: he reaches the window only from the corner at the bed's foot.
- **Bed:** a 160 × 200 double, white faux-leather frame with a tall tufted
  headboard against the long wall, the window at its side. White fitted
  sheet, two pillows with patterned covers, a dark grey fleece blanket
  (summer: no duvet).
- **Bedside:** two matching low white cabinets, a wood-look top over a drawer
  over a door, one on each side of the headboard; on each a table lamp with a
  rocker switch on its cord or foot, warm white. He sleeps on the door side,
  so his own lamp is the door-side one.
- **Not in the room:** no desk, computer, printer, wall cabinet or wardrobe in
  shot.
- **Light:** evening — the two lamps, warm (about 2700 K); night — dark, a
  faint orange glow of the street through the curtain; morning — grey daylight
  through the curtain, the lamp warm against it.

## Outside: dusk, night and dawn

- **Light presets** beside "sunny" and "rain": dusk (no sun, blue sky light,
  warm horizon), night (dark blue, very low sky light) and the dawn grey that
  fades into the existing rain look. The printed surfaces follow the day's
  light through the existing DayLight dimming; the rain haze applies from the
  dawn on.
- **Lit things:** flat windows that can glow warm (his, Karen's, a scattering
  of others with fixed, deterministic on/off times), the stairwell windows on
  the Treppenlicht timer, the entrance lamp on its sensor, the street lamps
  (warm-white LED, about 3000 K).
- **Roofs:** flat roofs with a low parapet on No. 12, No. 13 and the
  neighbouring blocks.
- **Town:** a low-detail silhouette of blocks beyond the street, mostly dark,
  with a few lit windows at night.
- **His window:** no roller shutter and no shutter box; the sand curtain
  behind the glass. This change shows in every scene that sees the facade.

## Tibik

- Walks with the shared gait (home along the pavement, in through the
  Haustür, round the bed, to the window).
- New movements: sitting on the bed's edge, lying down on his back, lying
  still (breathing stays a parked look decision), sitting up, getting up;
  pressing a lamp's rocker switch; drawing the blanket up and pushing it back;
  pulling the near curtain panel open by its edge.
- The blanket drapes over his lying body and the mattress; it never passes
  through him, and he never passes through the bed, the cabinets or the
  curtain.

## Sound

- **Dusk, outside:** the street ambience at evening level (fewer cars), a
  blackbird singing (free CC0/public-domain recording or synthesised; if
  neither exists, ask before choosing).
- **Night:** a distant traffic hum, near silence.
- **Bedroom:** the street muffled through the window (the shared indoor
  loops), the lamp switches' clicks, the fleece's rustle; morning: the rain on
  the glass (the shared rain, its indoor twin) and the curtain's gliders on
  the ceiling rail. The rain carries over the cut into the epilogue.
- Every file credited in public/sfx/CREDITS.md, as the existing test demands.

## Timeline and continuity

- Film order: Treppenhaus → Papiertonnen → Hausmuell → Klingelsturm →
  **Nachtruhe** → Rundschreiben.
- Declared time jumps: Klingelsturm → Nachtruhe (a few hours, afternoon to
  dusk); inside Nachtruhe the two weeks pass during the night (shot 3 → 4);
  Nachtruhe → Rundschreiben (morning to later that day). The epilogue's start
  state (Tibik on the pavement, the bins emptied, every lid shut) stays.
- The epilogue's caption moves here; its "caption is up for the first three
  seconds" check moves with it.
- The film gets about 29 s longer.

## Structure

- `src/scenes/nachtruhe/`: choreography, cameras, solve, sound, the scene
  component and its tests, following the other scenes.
- `src/building/`: the bedroom set and its shapes (pure data beside the

- `src/weather/`: the dusk, night and dawn presets alongside sunny and rain.

## Tests

As for every scene:

- **Nothing passes through anything, outlines included:** Tibik and the bed,
  blanket, cabinets, lamps, curtain and door; the drone camera and every roof,
  balcony, dish and wall on its path.
- **Cameras:** the lens is never inside or against a solid; the 180° rule
  across cuts; each shot's key action stays near the centre (9:16-friendly).
- **Continuity:** each shot starts where the last ended, except at the
  declared time jumps; Nachtruhe's first frame continues Klingelsturm's world
  (the parcel gone from his hands), its last frame leads into the epilogue.
- **Light order:** the entrance lamp, the stairwell and his window switch on
  in that order at dusk; in shot 2 only his own lamp is on, the far one stays
  off all night; his window is dark when shot 3 opens and Karen's goes out
  last.
- **Sound:** every file exists and is credited; the rain starts with the dawn
  and runs on into the epilogue.
- **Unchanged elsewhere:** the sunny scenes stay pixel-identical apart from
  his window losing its shutter.
- **Everyday imperfections:** reviewers check the new set and props against
  CONTINUITY's section and park new ideas.

## Out of scope

- The rest of his flat beyond the bedroom door (only glimpsed).
- The 9:16 reframe and the voices (later, user's call).
- Idle breathing (a parked look decision).
