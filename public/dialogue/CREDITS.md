Voices of the film, published with the owner's permission (2026-11-09).

de/ and en/ hold one finished take per line of docs/RECORDING_SCRIPT.md, named by its ID: Tibik (his inner voice
reading the letter included) and Karen are the owner's own recordings; the HWS answering machine (anrufbeantworter) and
the police dispatcher (leitstelle) were generated by the owner with ElevenLabs. The takes are cut, cleaned and limited,
with no effects.

The phone/, inner/ and far/ folders hold processed copies made from those takes by scripts/make-voices.mjs (the effects
are listed in src/dialogue/voiceEffects.json); src/dialogue/takes.json is measured from the takes by
scripts/measure-takes.mjs.
