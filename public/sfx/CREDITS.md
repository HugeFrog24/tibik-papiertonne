Sound effects in this folder are by Kenney (https://kenney.nl), released under
CC0 1.0 (public domain), from the "RPG Audio", "Impact Sounds" and "Interface
Sounds" (pluck_001.ogg, switch_002.ogg) packs; doorOpen_1.ogg, doorClose_1.ogg,
doorClose_4.ogg and metalLatch.ogg are from "RPG Audio".
Downloaded via https://github.com/kapishdima/soundcn/tree/main/assets.

street-atmosphere.wav, street-traffic.wav and street-leaves.wav, and their muffled versions
street-atmosphere-muffled.wav, street-traffic-muffled.wav and street-leaves-muffled.wav
(heard from inside the building), are generated by scripts/make-ambience.mjs
(synthesised for this project, no third-party material).

The files in stairwell/ are sounds from this folder (the Kenney sounds above and the phone/
sounds below) run through a synthetic stairwell reverb by scripts/make-stairwell.mjs; each keeps
its source's licence.

The files in phone/ are synthesised for this project by scripts/make-phone.mjs (no third-party
material): the doorbell, the DTMF keypad tones, the German line tones (ringback and busy, 425 Hz),
the answering machine's beep, the receiver click and Karen's panting (filtered noise).

The files in flur/ other than tape-pull.wav are Kenney sounds above (footstep_concrete_000.ogg to
footstep_concrete_003.ogg, bookPlace1.ogg, impactSoft_heavy_000.ogg) as heard through a flat door,
made by scripts/make-flur.mjs (CC0 like their sources).

street-pigeons.wav and street-pigeons-muffled.wav are synthesised by scripts/make-ambience.mjs from a
model that scripts/learn-pigeons.mjs learns from two public-domain wood pigeon recordings on
Wikimedia Commons, kept in assets/recordings/:
"Ringeltaube (ruft)" by Jens Kraglund (Sleepytomcat),
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ringeltaube_(ruft).ogg, and
"Wood pigeon call 128" by Crunchysaviour,
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wood_pigeon_call_128.ogg.

fly-buzz-0.wav, fly-buzz-1.wav and fly-buzz-2.wav are synthesised by scripts/make-flies.mjs from a
model that scripts/learn-flies.mjs learns from the author's own house fly recordings (kept locally,
not distributed).

phone/bell-storm.wav and phone/bell-storm-door.wav are a synthesised 680 Hz square-wave doorbell played in a
2-3-4-2 rhythm that scripts/learn-rhythm.mjs learns from the author's own clapped recording (kept locally,
not distributed).

flur/tape-pull.wav is "Adhesive tape #3" by Denis Chardonnet, BigSoundBank, CC0 (public domain),
https://bigsoundbank.com/adhesive-tape-3-s0299.html, trimmed by scripts/make-tape.mjs.

The Kenney sounds used by the scenes, by file: bookFlip1.ogg, bookFlip2.ogg, bookFlip3.ogg,
bookOpen.ogg, bookPlace1.ogg, cloth2.ogg, creak1.ogg, doorClose_1.ogg, doorClose_4.ogg,
doorOpen_1.ogg, footstep_concrete_000.ogg, footstep_concrete_001.ogg, footstep_concrete_002.ogg,
footstep_concrete_003.ogg, handleCoins.ogg,
metalClick.ogg, metalLatch.ogg ("RPG Audio" and "Impact Sounds"), impactGeneric_light_002.ogg and
impactSoft_heavy_000.ogg ("Impact Sounds"), pluck_001.ogg and switch_002.ogg ("Interface Sounds").
handleCoins.ogg and metalClick.ogg were added for the Rundschreiben (keys, the mailbox lock and the
Haustür's cylinder).
cloth1.ogg, cloth3.ogg, cloth4.ogg, creak2.ogg and creak3.ogg ("RPG Audio") and footstep_wood_000.ogg,
footstep_wood_001.ogg, footstep_wood_002.ogg and footstep_wood_003.ogg ("Impact Sounds") were added for
Nachtruhe (the fleece, the curtain's fabric, the bed and his steps on the bedroom's laminate).

rain.wav and rain-muffled.wav (heard from inside the building) are made by scripts/make-rain.mjs from
"Rain" by ezwa, public domain (released worldwide by its author, originally on PDSounds.org),
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain_(1).ogg, kept in assets/recordings/rain-ezwa.ogg: its
steadiest 32 s are crossfaded into a 30 s loop, the few close drop crackles softened (4:1 above the
99.99th percentile), and the muffled twin filtered like the street ambience.

Music: public/music/mainstreet.flac ("mainstreet", 2:24, stereo FLAC 44.1 kHz / 24 bit), supplied by
the film's author for this film and vendored as is. It plays under Treppenhaus, Papiertonnen and the
start of Hausmuell (src/sound/music.ts).

blackbird.wav and blackbird-muffled.wav (heard through his bedroom window) are made by
scripts/make-blackbird.mjs from "blackbirds at dawn.wav" by squashy555, CC0 1.0 (public domain),
https://freesound.org/people/squashy555/sounds/341675/ (a Tascam DR-07 recording of a blackbird singing
in the dawn chorus, UK, March 2016); its first 30 s are kept in
assets/recordings/blackbirds-at-dawn-squashy555.ogg, and 17.5 s of them (one blackbird's phrases and
pauses, a few distant birds behind) are faded in and out for the dusk.

curtain-gliders.wav is synthesised for this project by scripts/make-curtain.mjs (no third-party
material): the plastic gliders of his curtain's ceiling track running along the rail and clicking
into one another as the panel gathers, over the 1.2 s he draws it.
